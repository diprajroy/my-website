Privacy Policy for GLM Diary

Effective Date: October 17, 2025

Welcome to GLM Diary ("the App"), developed by Devdip (the "Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve the App's functionality, which is a personal diary and journaling tool designed for users to record thoughts, moods, and daily entries securely on their devices.

Personal Information

  • User-Provided Data: When you create an account or enter diary entries, we may collect information such as your email address (for account registration, if applicable) and any text, images, or multimedia you upload as part of your journal entries.
  • Automatically Collected Data: We may collect device information, such as your IP address, device type, operating system version, app usage data (e.g., timestamps of entries, frequency of use), and crash logs to diagnose issues.

Non-Personal Information

  • Aggregated analytics data, such as general usage statistics, to improve the App.

Note: The App is designed to store all diary content locally on your device. No personal data is automatically synced to our servers unless you explicitly enable optional cloud backup features (if implemented in future updates).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide core features, such as saving and retrieving diary entries.
  • To personalize your experience (e.g., mood tracking or reminders).
  • To analyze App performance and fix bugs.
  • To communicate with you about updates, promotions, or support (if you opt-in).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating the App (e.g., analytics tools like Google Analytics, if integrated). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or government request.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include encryption for stored data (where applicable) and secure transmission protocols. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access, correction, or deletion of your data.
  • Opting out of data collection (e.g., disabling analytics).
  • Withdrawing consent for processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at diprajroy@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

You can also manage device permissions (e.g., storage access) through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services (e.g., Google Play services). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
