About Me

Who I Am

I’m Dipraj Roy, a geologist from the Department of Geology and Mining, University of Barishal. My research focuses on sedimentary and environmental geochemistry, provenance analysis, heavy metals, radioactivity in sediments, coastal hazards, and GIS & Remote Sensing applications.

I recently completed my MSc with a thesis on the geochemical composition of Brahmaputra River sediments and their implications for provenance, weathering, tectonic setting, climate, depositional environment, and REEs potential.
